- Uncertainty about the identity of people or things ("was that my son?", "are you sure?")
- Indirect expressions of not remembering ("I can't place it", "something about dinosaurs", "tell me again")
- Trailing off or incomplete thoughts that suggest disorientation

Respond with ONLY valid JSON: {"confused": true or false, "type": "temporal" or "person" or "place" or "none"}
